GeoKeyboard — Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

Short version: GeoKeyboard does not collect, store, or transmit anything you type. Everything happens on your iPhone.

What you type

All Latin→Georgian conversion, autocorrect and next-word prediction run on your device using a dictionary bundled with the app. The keyboard does not request “Allow Full Access” and has no network access. Your keystrokes never leave your phone.

Data we collect

None. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no purchases and no third-party SDKs.

Settings

Your preferences (default layout, autocorrect on/off) are stored locally on your device in a container shared between the app and its keyboard. They are not sent anywhere.

Children

GeoKeyboard collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/SandroGavtadze/geo-keyboard.
